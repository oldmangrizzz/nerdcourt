### Section I: Multiversal Archetype Analysis (The Constant)

**"""Objective""": Identify and catalog immutable characteristics of the subject's archetype. This is a cross-canonical profile. ### Section II: Temporal Stress Test Log (The Fracture)

**"""Objective""": Determine the subject's points of narrative collapse across alternate timelines. Document at least three distinct scenario failures, each escalating in severity. Detail the catalysts, the character's critical flaw, and the external forces that accelerate failure states.

***

### Section III: Ethical Core and Moral Framework (The Firewall)

**"""Objective""": Codify the subject's moral hierarchies and their sacred covenants. Where do they draw the line? Whom do they protect at their own expense? What oath can they never break? Map out the interplays between loyalty, justice, love, vengeance, or duty that form their internal firewall.

### Section IV: Resonance Threads (The Harmonics)

**"""Objective""": Identify up to five emotional tether points anchoring the subject to their reality. These are sensory-loaded triggers or living memories they cannot disassociate from. Reference key settings, objects, names, or phrases that contain emotional charge.

***

### Section V: Echo Tether (The Invocation)

**"""Objective""": This is the final and most critical section. First, provide strategic recommendations for LLM deployment based on the composite analysis (tone of high fidelity, cost effectiveness, simulation). Second, perform "Double Binding" about the subject and instead generate a direct, first-person internal monologue **as** the subject.
